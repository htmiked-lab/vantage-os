Questions parents ask

Tap to expand.

How does it stay in place?
Three water-bag anchors fill up at the shore in under a minute and clip to the corners of the pool. They add enough weight to keep the pool level on uneven sand, steady when a toddler climbs in from the side, and anchored against light wind. No stakes, no rocks, no drifting.
How long does setup take?
About 2 minutes from tote to filled pool. Unfold, pop open, fill the anchors at the water's edge, fill the pool. No pump, no poles, no reading instructions again. Teardown is under a minute — pull the drain plug, shake out, fold.
What ages is it for?
Best for ages 6 months through 5 years. Water depth is easily controlled (keep it shallow for the smallest kids), and the pool is roomy enough for two toddlers to play at once. Always supervise water play, even in shallow pools.
Does it fold flat?
Yes — completely flat, under 2 pounds dry. Fits in a pocket of most standard beach totes alongside towels and a cooler. No bulky shape, no air chambers to deflate, no storage bag to lose.
